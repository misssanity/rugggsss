# Rugggsss

Sweep your mess under the rug. A cozy cloth-physics rug for your Mac desktop.

**Website:** https://misssanity.github.io/rugggsss/
**Download:** [Rugggsss.dmg](https://github.com/misssanity/rugggsss/releases/latest/download/Rugggsss.dmg) (macOS, Apple Silicon)

## Inspiration
Rugggsss is an independent, fan-made take on [Terkel's viral desktop rug](https://x.com/terkelg/status/2107540718461886464). His rug was such a delightful idea that I wanted one of my own. It isn't his app and isn't affiliated with him, so go give the original some love.

Made by Yelizaveta, for herself first, and now for you.
